Open WebUI: a real chat for your local AI

Two commands and the models you already run with Ollama get a clean chat in your browser. It runs on your PC, even offline.

You need first: Ollama with at least one model and Python 3.12 (from python.org).

  1. Open PowerShell and install Open WebUI: 
    pip install open-webui
  2. Start it: 
    open-webui serve
  3. Open your browser at http://localhost:8080, create your user (it stays on your PC) and pick the model at the top.

The first start is slow: it downloads what it needs. To stop it, press Ctrl+C in the PowerShell window.

The project: github.com/open-webui/open-webui
